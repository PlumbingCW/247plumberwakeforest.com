Disposal hums but will not turn, or leaks underneath

A humming disposal with no spin is jammed. The usual cause is a spoon, bone or fibrous peel wedged between the flywheel and the shredder ring. A plumber can free it with a hex key at the bottom of the unit, or remove the blockage from above using tongs with the power off.

If the disposal is leaking, the location tells the story. A leak at the top flange or the sink gasket is a seal problem. A leak at the drain connection is a loose or cracked pipe. A leak at the bottom of the housing means the internal seal has failed, and that is the end of the unit. It must be replaced.

Why Raleigh Water asks you to keep grease out of the disposal

Raleigh Water's fats, oils and grease program says even when a grease trap is present, disposals are not designed to handle grease. It tells residents to wipe pans with a paper towel and throw the grease in the trash. A disposal grinds food into smaller pieces, but grease and starch still coat the pipe wall downstream.

That is why a disposal and a clogged kitchen line often go together. If your sink slows down after months of use, a cleaning through drain cleaning may fix the line, while the disposal itself is fine.

Installing or replacing a disposal

A replacement disposal comes in a few power ratings, from about half a horsepower to a full horsepower. Half to three quarters of a horsepower handles most households, and a higher rating runs quieter and jams less. A new unit needs an electrical connection, either a plug-in cord to an outlet under the sink or a hardwired connection to a switch.

If a dishwasher is present, the drain hose connects to the disposal. A knockout plug inside the disposal inlet must be removed before the hose is connected, or the dishwasher will not drain. It is a common mistake with do-it-yourself installs and we check it every time.

Safety before you touch a jammed disposal

A jammed disposal is a machine with a motor and blades. Do these in order.

Switch off the wall switch

Then unplug the unit or turn off its breaker so it cannot start.

Never reach in by hand

Use tongs or pliers to lift out anything you can see.

Use the hex key at the bottom

Many units have a socket underneath that turns the plates by hand.

Press the reset button

Wait a few minutes for a tripped overload to cool before pressing it.

Skip chemical drain cleaners

They can splash back and harm the unit and the person using it.

How do I unjam a garbage disposal?

Turn off the power at the switch and the breaker. Insert a hex key in the socket at the bottom of the unit and turn it back and forth until the flywheel moves freely. Remove any object you can see with tongs, then press the red reset button.

Can a leaking garbage disposal be repaired?

Leaks at the flange or drain connection can be repaired. A leak from the bottom or side of the housing means the internal seal is gone and the disposal must be replaced.

What should never go into a disposal?

Grease, oil, coffee grounds in large amounts, bones, fibrous peels, pasta and rice in quantity, and anything that is not food. Raleigh Water says disposals are not designed to handle grease.
