Repair or replace: how to decide

The tank's condition decides it. A leak from the tank body means the steel has failed and replacement is the only fix. A failed element or thermostat on a young tank is a repair. The gray area is a heater past about ten years old with a repairable fault, where a new part may be followed by a new failure within months.

Ask the plumber to show you the data plate. The first four digits of the serial number commonly encode the year of manufacture, and the rating label shows capacity and fuel. A heater near the end of its range, with rust at the base or a corroded fitting, is usually better replaced. Start with water heater repair if the problem is a simple part failure.

Choosing the replacement: tank, tankless or hybrid

A like-for-like gas or electric tank is the quickest and least expensive swap, because the connections, venting and electrical or gas supply already match. A 40 or 50 gallon tank suits most small and mid-size households, and a larger tank helps a family with several bathrooms.

A tankless unit heats water on demand and frees floor space, but it needs the right gas line size or electrical capacity and a venting plan. Our tankless water heater page covers that choice in detail. Hybrid heat pump heaters use less electricity but need clearance and a drain.

What a same-day replacement involves

A tank swap follows a set order, and nothing is closed up until it has been checked.

  1. Shut down and drain

    The old tank is isolated, drained and disconnected, and the area is protected.

  2. Set and connect the new unit

    The new heater is set level on a drain pan where required and connected with new flex lines or code-approved piping.

  3. Relief valve and expansion

    A temperature and pressure relief valve with a proper discharge pipe is installed. A thermal expansion tank is added where the plumbing is a closed system.

  4. Venting, gas or power

    Gas units get a venting check and a leak test. Electric units get a power check at the proper breaker.

  5. Test and walk-through

    The plumber tests temperature, checks for leaks and shows you the shutoffs.

What changes the price of a replacement

Heaters come in many sizes and fuel types. These are the factors that set the final number.

CapacityA 40 gallon tank costs less than an 80 gallon one, and rising demand for hot water may push you up a size.
LocationA garage unit is easy to swap. An attic or tight closet takes more time and sometimes more parts.
