A camera on a flexible cable shows the inside of the sewer line. You see the roots, cracks and sags before paying for a repair.

A sewer camera inspection puts a waterproof camera on the end of a flexible cable and sends it through your sewer line. This page covers sewer camera inspection in Wake Forest, NC, for repeat backups, home purchases and before major repairs.

If sewage is coming up now, it is not a planned visit. Call the 24/7 line or read sewer line cleaning for the first steps. If you want to understand what is happening inside the line, a camera inspection is the tool.

What the camera shows

The camera records the pipe wall as it passes. Roots appear as fibrous masses at joints. Cracks show as dark lines, offset joints as steps, and a belly as a stretch where water pools. Scale and grease show as a rough wall. A collapsed pipe shows as a blocked view.

A locator tracks the camera head from the surface, so the plumber can mark the depth and position of any problem on the ground. That precision limits digging to the right spot.

Who owns what, and what the camera can settle

Raleigh Water's homeowner guidance says the homeowner maintains the sewer service from the house to the city sewer. It says that if a camera inspection finds damage in the street portion, the owner should contact Raleigh Public Utilities, whose staff will visit and verify with your plumber whether repairs are needed. It also says less than 1.0% of backups are found to be the city's responsibility.

A video that shows where the damage sits, and its distance from the cleanout, makes that conversation specific. Save the recording.

Limits of the camera

A camera cannot pass a complete collapse, and it cannot see through standing water in a belly. A line that is heavily blocked has to be cleared first, which adds a step. We say so up front, so you are not surprised if the first visit includes cleaning. See hydro jetting for lines that need scouring before the camera goes in.

After the inspection

You get a recording and a plain explanation of what we saw. If the pipe is sound, you are done. If it is damaged, we walk through the options and the price, including whether lining, bursting or open-cut repair fits. The sewer line repair page explains each method.

Questions about sewer camera inspection in Wake Forest

How long does a sewer camera inspection take?

A straightforward inspection of a clear line usually takes an hour or so. Lines that need cleaning first, or long and complex runs, take longer.

Can the camera find a leak?

It can show cracks, offset joints and signs of water entering or leaving the pipe. A leak in a supply line is found with leak detection equipment, not a sewer camera.

Should I get an inspection before buying a house?

For an older house, yes. A failing lateral is expensive to repair, and a camera inspection before closing gives you information to negotiate.
