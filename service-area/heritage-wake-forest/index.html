Heritage, Wake Forest

Plumber in Heritage, Wake Forest, NC

Heritage was built in phases, so houses vary in age. A nearby subdivision sits partly in Rolesville, and we serve both towns.

Heritage is one of the larger planned communities south of downtown Wake Forest, along Rogers Road and Heritage Lake Road. It was built in phases over more than a decade, and a neighboring subdivision named Regency at Heritage has one phase inside Rolesville. This page explains what that means for utilities, permits and the plumbing in houses that are now many years old.

What the local record says

A local report on Wake Forest subdivisions says development of Heritage began in 2000 under Ammons Development Group and that its last section opened in 2012. It describes the project as mixed-use. The Town's Dunn Creek Greenway page places the Smith Creek Soccer Center at 690 Heritage Lake Road, where the greenway begins.

Heritage and the Rolesville line

The Town of Rolesville's page for Regency at Heritage says phases one and two of that subdivision are in Wake Forest, and 27 lots in phase three are inside Rolesville's corporate limits. The county line can change which office issues permits. Our Rolesville page covers that side.

What 20 years does to a house's plumbing

The earliest Heritage houses date to the start of development in 2000, so some are now more than two decades old. General experience with houses of this age is that original water heaters have reached or passed the end of their expected life, and rubber parts in toilets and faucets have worn. Whether your home is original or replaced is something we check on site.

A house from the latest phase, finished around 2012, is more than a decade old. Its first major plumbing events, such as a heater replacement or a failing disposal, are likely arriving about now.

Which office handles your permit

For a Heritage address inside the Town of Wake Forest, the Inspections Department at 301 S. Brooks St. handles permits at 919-435-9531. For a lot inside Rolesville, the Town of Rolesville says permits are reviewed through the Wake County Permit Portal, and county customer service is 919-856-6222.

If you do not know which side of the line your address is on, ask us when you call. We will tell you where to check before any permit is filed.

Planning ahead in a house that is no longer new

The cheapest repair is usually the one done before a failure. Look at the base of the water heater for rust or a damp patch, and note the date on its label. Test the main shutoff once a year so it turns freely. A valve that has not moved in a decade can seize at the moment you need it.

If a heater is near the end of its life, replacing it on your own schedule costs less than replacing it after a leak. See water heater installation for what a planned swap involves. These habits apply to any house of this age and are not a diagnosis of yours.

Heritage plumbing questions

Do you serve all of Heritage?

Yes, including the parts near the Rolesville line. Call (919) 944-7281 with your street address and the dispatcher will schedule a plumber.

Is my Heritage address in Wake Forest or Rolesville?

A neighboring subdivision, Regency at Heritage, has one phase inside Rolesville, so the town line runs close by. The mailing address does not settle it, so check with the Town or with Wake County.

My water heater is about 20 years old. Should I replace it now?

Age alone is not a failure, but an old tank can leak without warning. A plumber can check the tank and the surrounding area and tell you whether repair or replacement makes sense.

Who owns the pipe between the meter and my house?

The homeowner. Raleigh Water repairs the main and the connection to the meter, at 919-996-3245. Everything after the meter is your responsibility.
