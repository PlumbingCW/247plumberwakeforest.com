Rolesville at a glance

Incorporated 1837

The North Carolina Legislature incorporated Rolesville on January 18, 1837. Older properties near the town center sit alongside much newer houses, so a plumber meets very different pipe in one afternoon.

9,475 residents in 2020

The 2020 census counted 9,475 people. A town that size needs plumbing help at all hours, so emergency dispatch for Rolesville is open around the clock.

Fast growth

Wake County Economic Development reports Rolesville grew 150 percent between 2010 and 2020 and was the fastest-growing North Carolina municipality among towns of 5,000 or more in 2020.

Raleigh Water service

The Town contracts with the City of Raleigh for water, sewer and irrigation. Customer service is 919-996-3245, and our 24/7 line handles the plumbing on your side of the meter.

What newer housing means for a plumbing call

Much of Rolesville's housing is recent, given the growth since 2010, and that changes which problems a plumber meets first. In a house that new, the water heater is often still the original. The early service calls tend to be a failing first heater, a poorly sealed toilet or a slow drain that has built up soap and hair.

Newer homes also tend to have more built-in features. Irrigation systems, tankless heaters, whole-house shutoffs and garbage disposals each add a point of failure. Rolesville's building permit guide says irrigation requires an additional permit through Raleigh Public Utilities, and a leaking irrigation line shows up as a high water bill rather than a puddle. Our leak detection crews trace those with a meter test.

Where a plumber starts in a newer town

A water heater that has stopped heating is a natural first suspect in a town of newer houses, because first heaters tend to reach the end of their life around the same time. See water heater repair for what we check first, and water heater replacement if the tank has failed.

Drains come next. A kitchen line that fills with grease or a shower that stops draining is a clog we clear through drain cleaning. Third are fixtures, such as a toilet that runs or a faucet that will not shut off, covered by toilet repair and faucet repair.

Before we arrive: if you have a burst pipe or an active leak

These steps limit damage while the plumber is on the way. They take a few minutes and apply to most houses in Rolesville.

Shut off the main water valve

It is usually where the line enters the house, or at the meter box near the street. Turn it fully clockwise.

Kill power to wet areas

Switch off the breaker for any room with water near outlets, lights or appliances. Stay out if the floor around the panel is wet.

Clear the area

Move rugs, boxes and furniture out of the water's path. Photograph the damage before you move things.

Open a low faucet

Draining the lines relieves pressure and reduces what leaks out of the break.

Know your landmark

Tell the dispatcher your street and the nearest landmark, such as Town Hall on Southtown Circle or Main Street Park. It helps the crew find the house.

Water, sewer and permits: who handles what

Three offices touch most Rolesville plumbing jobs. Raleigh Water runs the water and sewer system and takes calls at 919-996-3245. Wake County Building Permits and Inspections handles trade permits and inspections, at 919-856-6222. The Town of Rolesville handles zoning and other town permits from Town Hall at 502 Southtown Circle.

A plumber also works inside those boundaries. A permit applies to a plumbing installation, extension, relocation or replacement under Wake County's guidance, and it exempts minor fixture repairs that do not disturb supply, waste or vent lines. Our crews tell you which side of that line your job falls on.

Rock, roads and what it does to a repair

Rolesville sits on a rock body known as the Rolesville Pluton, and rocky outcrops are visible within the town limits. Rock close to the surface can slow trenching for a service line or sewer repair, so an excavator should check before quoting a long run. A trenchless method may avoid some of that. Our water service line page covers the options.

After Raleigh Water works on a main

Raleigh Water posts maintenance notices for Rolesville neighborhoods. A 2023 notice covered main filling and flushing at The Point development on East Young Street and maintenance in the Kalas Falls area. After main work, a house can see discolored or sputtering water for a short time. Run a cold tap until it clears before using the water heater.

If the water stays discolored or pressure stays low after the notice period, call Raleigh Water, and call us if you suspect the problem is inside the house. A sewer line cleaning visit covers the drain side.

Parks, Town Hall and giving directions

Rolesville's parks include Main Street Park, Mill Bridge Nature Park and Redford Place Park. Many new subdivisions have similar-looking streets, so when you call, say which park or the Town Hall is nearest. That detail saves minutes when an address is hard to find at night.

Rolesville plumbing questions

Is emergency service available in Rolesville?

Yes. The dispatch line at (919) 944-7281 is open 24 hours a day, every day, and Rolesville calls go to the same line as Wake Forest. Describe the problem, give your street address and the nearest landmark, and the dispatcher books the next available plumber.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Rolesville?

Rolesville is served by the 27571 ZIP code, which covers the town, including the area around Main Street and Southtown Circle and the newer neighborhoods. We also take calls from the neighboring 27587 ZIP code in Wake Forest, to the west, and the nearby 27614 ZIP code in north Raleigh.

If your address sits near a ZIP boundary and you are not sure we reach it, give the dispatcher your street and they will tell you.

Who handles plumbing permits for a Rolesville home?

Wake County provides permits and inspections for Rolesville, and the Town's permit guide says applications are reviewed and issued through the Wake County Permit Portal. Wake County Building Permits and Inspections customer service is 919-856-6222.

Water, sewer and irrigation work needs an additional permit through the City of Raleigh Public Utilities permit portal. Our crews tell you when a job needs a permit and which office issues it.

Who do I call for a water main problem in Rolesville?

Rolesville contracts with the City of Raleigh for water, sewer and irrigation service, so a main break in the street is a call to Raleigh Water at 919-996-3245. A leak between your meter and your house, or inside it, is a plumbing call to us.

If you are not sure which side of the meter the problem sits on, call us and we will tell you.
