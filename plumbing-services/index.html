Plumbing Services in Wake Forest, NC

Twenty plumbing services from one 24/7 phone line, from emergency repairs to planned installations.

Call (919) 944-7281

Every service on this page is available through one phone number, (919) 944-7281. Some are emergencies that cannot wait, and others are planned projects you can book for a day that suits you. The groups below explain how the services fit together.

Emergencies that cannot wait

Water on the floor is the clearest emergency. A pipe that has split needs burst pipe repair, and a stain or a meter that will not stop points to leak detection. A buried leak between the meter and the house is a water service line repair.

A leak that began at a toilet, faucet or disposal is usually a fixture problem. Our toilet repair, faucet repair and garbage disposal pages cover the common faults.

Drains and sewer lines

A single slow sink is a job for drain cleaning. When water backs up in several places, the main line is the issue and sewer line cleaning clears it to the city sewer. Grease and roots that keep returning call for hydro jetting.

If the line is broken or collapsed, sewer line repair is the lasting answer, and a sewer camera inspection shows you the damage first. These pages all draw on Raleigh Water's rules for who owns which part of the line.

Not sure which service you need? Call and describe the problem.

Call (919) 944-7281

Water heaters

No hot water and a leaking tank are different problems. Water heater repair fixes failed parts, and water heater replacement swaps a tank that has failed. A planned change is a job for water heater installation, and units that heat water on demand are covered under tankless water heater service.

Planned projects and whole-house work

When old pipe keeps failing, repiping replaces the supply lines. New bathrooms, kitchens and fixtures are covered by plumbing installation. Businesses can use our commercial plumbing crews for after-hours repairs and planned work.

How pricing and permits work

You get a clear price before work starts, and you decide whether to go ahead. Each service page ends with a cost section that lists what moves the number, such as access, pipe material and the time of the call. Prices differ with the job, so we do not print a flat rate that would mislead you.

Some work needs a permit. Wake Forest runs its own Inspections Department at 919-435-9531, and Wake County handles permits for towns such as Rolesville. Our crews are licensed and insured, and you can check any plumber through the state board's license search.

Which page should you read first?

Start with the symptom. Water where it should not be belongs with the emergency pages. A fixture that works badly belongs with the fixture pages. A project you are planning belongs with the installation pages. If none of these fit, call the 24/7 line and describe what you see. The dispatcher can point you to the right service in a minute.

Water in the basement or crawl space

A failed pump during a storm needs sump pump service. If you want a lasting fix for a wet foundation, basement waterproofing covers drainage systems. Wake Forest's flood-prone streets make this a local concern, and the Town's flood hazard page helps you check your property's risk.
