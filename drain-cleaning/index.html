A drain that has stopped completely, or one that gurgles and pushes water back up, is an emergency in a house with one bathroom. This is emergency drain cleaning in Wake Forest, NC, and the 24/7 line is open tonight, on weekends and on holidays.

While you wait, stop running water in the house and pull the plug from any tub or sink that is holding water. Skip the store-bought chemical openers. They sit in a standing blockage, they can burn skin when a plumber opens the trap, and they do nothing for a clog made of hair and soap.

Backups that cannot wait until morning

Treat a blockage as urgent when more than one fixture is affected, when a toilet gurgles after the shower runs, or when water or sewage appears in a floor drain. Those signs point to the main line rather than a single trap. A main-line backup keeps filling every time someone flushes or runs a tap.

A single slow sink is different and can wait for a booked visit. If you are not sure which you have, call and describe the sounds and the fixtures involved. The dispatcher can usually tell you which case it is. Wake Forest homes with a basement or a low floor drain feel main-line backups first, because the drain is the lowest opening in the house.

Kitchen sink clogs and what Raleigh Water asks of homeowners

Kitchen drains clog differently from bathroom drains. The usual culprit is grease and food residue that coats the pipe wall and narrows it a little more with each meal. Raleigh Water's fats, oils and grease program tells residents to wipe pans and plates with a paper towel and put the grease in the trash, and it notes that disposals are not designed to handle grease.

That advice matters here because Raleigh Water runs the sewer system for Wake Forest. Grease that clears your trap can still harden further down and cause a backup in the line. If a kitchen drain keeps returning after a cleaning, the pipe may need hydro jetting, which scours the wall rather than punching a hole through the buildup.

Bathroom drains, showers and tubs

Hair is the main cause in showers and tubs, and a tub or shower drain that has stopped can often be cleared in one visit. Floor drains and laundry standpipes clog on lint and soap. If the shower fills while the sink drains fine, the problem is in the shared branch and a snake through the tub drain usually reaches it.

Water that backs up in a tub when you run the washer means the blockage is lower in the system than the fixtures themselves. That points toward the main line and the sewer line cleaning service rather than a bathroom fix.

Repeat clogs and what the camera shows

A line that blocks every few months is telling you something. Common causes are tree roots entering a joint, a belly where the pipe has sagged, grease build-up, or a section of old pipe that has started to flake. A sewer camera inspection shows which one it is, so you pay to fix the cause once rather than clearing the symptom again and again.

The sewer service between your house and the city main is the homeowner's responsibility under Raleigh Water's guidance, and the city says a cleanout should sit at the property line. If yours is missing, installing one makes every later cleaning cheaper and faster. If the camera shows a crack or a collapse, the answer moves to sewer line repair.

How do I clear a clogged drain without chemicals?

Start with a plunger sized for the fixture and seal the overflow opening with a wet rag. A hand-turned drain snake reaches hair clogs that a plunger will not. If more than one fixture is slow, stop and call, because the blockage is lower than you can reach.

Why does my kitchen sink keep clogging?

Grease and food residue build up on the pipe wall and narrow it over time. Wipe pans with a paper towel before washing and keep grease out of the disposal, as Raleigh Water advises residents. If clogs return within months, ask about hydro jetting.

Who is responsible for the sewer line between my house and the street?
