A sump pump does its work out of sight until the day it stops. When a storm drives groundwater up and the pump will not run, water can reach finished floors and stored belongings quickly. This is emergency sump pump repair in Wake Forest, NC, and the 24/7 line is open when it rains.

While you wait, check the power. Make sure the pump is plugged in and the breaker or GFCI outlet has not tripped. If the pit is filling, move stored items off the floor, turn off power to anything the water might reach and use a wet vacuum or bucket to buy time.

Sump pump failed in a storm: what to check

A few checks can tell you whether the pump has lost power or has failed.

  1. Check power

    Confirm the pump is plugged in and the outlet, GFCI and breaker are on. A tripped outlet is a common cause.

  2. Check the float

    Lift the float by hand. If the pump starts, the float was stuck or the switch is worn.

  3. Listen for the motor

    A humming pump that does not move water may have a jammed impeller or a failed motor.

  4. Check the discharge

    Water should leave the house through the discharge pipe. A frozen, kinked or clogged line sends it back.

  5. Call dispatch

    If the pump still does not run, a plumber can replace it.

Installing a new sump pump

A new installation starts with the pit. A plumber checks that the pit is deep enough, sets the pump on a stable base, installs a check valve on the discharge line and runs the discharge pipe away from the foundation. A check valve keeps water from flowing back into the pit when the pump shuts off, and the discharge should end well away from the house so water does not return.

A battery backup adds protection when a storm takes out the power, which is exactly when pumps are needed. Test the pump twice a year by pouring a bucket of water into the pit and checking that it starts, pumps and shuts off.

When the answer is more than a pump

A pump that runs constantly, or a basement that takes water at the wall rather than the pit, may need more drainage work. Basement waterproofing covers interior drain systems that route water to the pit. A pump is the last step in the system, and not every wet basement needs one.

How do I know if my sump pump is working?

Pour a bucket of water into the pit. The float should rise, the pump should start, empty the pit and shut off. If any of that does not happen, the pump needs attention.

How long does a sump pump last?

Many pumps last around 7 to 10 years depending on how often they run. A pump that cycles frequently may wear out sooner.
