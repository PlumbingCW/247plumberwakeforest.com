Sewer backup: what to do first

A backup is a signal. If sewage rises in the lowest drain in the house, the blockage or break is in the shared line below the fixtures. Clearing it is step one, and finding why it formed is step two. Raleigh Water's sewer FAQ says to call 919-996-3245 to report a backup, and its homeowner responsibility brochure says less than 1.0% of service backups are found to be the city's to correct.

That figure means most backups in Wake Forest are on the private side, between the house and the main. Our crews clear the line, camera it and tell you whose pipe it is. If the camera shows damage in the street portion, Raleigh Water will visit and verify with your plumber.

Who owns which part of the line

Raleigh Water's homeowner guidance says the owner maintains the sanitary sewer service from the house to the city sewer, in the street or an easement, and that the whole service should be cleaned in its entirety. It says a cleanout should sit at the property line, and that a missing one needs to be installed.

That clarity helps when a plumber claims they cannot clean the street portion. They can, and the page says the full length belongs to the homeowner. Knowing this up front shapes how you read any quote for repair.

How we find the break

A camera on a push cable goes down the line and records its condition. Cracks, offset joints, roots, sags and collapsed sections all show on the monitor, along with the distance from the cleanout. The plumber marks the location above ground with a locator so excavation, if needed, is aimed and small. See our sewer camera inspection page for what the camera shows and why it is worth doing before a big repair.

A backup that clears and returns within weeks is the typical sign of a broken pipe rather than a clog. If the line only needs cleaning, that is handled through sewer line cleaning.

Cast iron, clay and older lines

Older homes in the historic districts near downtown, such as the Glen Royall Mill Village houses built between 1900 and 1949, may still have original materials such as clay or cast iron laterals. Cast iron corrodes from the inside and flakes, and clay joints let roots in. We cannot see the pipe without a camera, so we do not guess.

Cast iron pipe that is rough inside but intact can sometimes be lined. A section that has rotted through is cut out and replaced with PVC. In either case the camera findings decide, not the age of the house.

Permits, code and who signs off

A new section of sewer line is a plumbing repair governed by the North Carolina State Building Code: Plumbing Code, and the Town's Inspections Department at 919-435-9531 can say whether your job needs a permit and an inspection. Ask for a camera recording after the repair so the new section is documented.

Questions about sewer line repair in Wake Forest

Do you handle sewer line emergencies at night or on weekends in Wake Forest?

Yes. Call (919) 944-7281 any hour. Sewage in a tub, shower or floor drain is an emergency, and the dispatcher books the next available plumber. You hear a clear price for the repair before any work starts.

How do I know if my sewer line is broken or just clogged?

A clog usually clears and stays clear. A broken line backs up again, often within weeks, and may show wet or sunken ground in the yard or a sewer smell outdoors. A camera inspection settles it.

Does Raleigh Water repair my sewer line?

The city repairs its main in the street. Raleigh Water's homeowner guidance says the owner maintains the service from the house to the main. If a camera finds damage in the street portion, call the city and they will visit with your plumber.

Can a sewer line be repaired without digging up the yard?

Sometimes. Pipe lining and pipe bursting avoid long trenches when the pipe is not collapsed. A camera inspection tells us whether trenchless methods fit.
