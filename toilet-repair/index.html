An overflowing toilet is an emergency in any house with one bathroom, and a toilet that leaks at the base can rot a subfloor quietly. This is emergency toilet repair in Wake Forest, NC, and the 24/7 line is open when the bowl will not stop rising.

While you wait, take the tank lid off and push the flapper down to stop the flow, or turn the shutoff valve behind the toilet clockwise. Lay towels around the base and keep the lid on the bowl until a plumber arrives.

Overflowing, running or leaking: three different jobs

An overflowing toilet is a clog or a venting problem. A toilet that runs on is a worn flapper, a failed fill valve or a chain that is too short. A leak at the base means the wax ring has failed or the bolts have loosened. Each has a different fix, and a plumber sorts them in a few minutes.

If a toilet is clogged and plunging does nothing, a closet auger reaches what a plunger will not. When the clog is not in the toilet at all but in the line behind it, the answer is drain cleaning and, when sewage rises elsewhere, sewer line cleaning.

What not to flush in Raleigh Water's sewer system

Raleigh Water's sewer FAQ asks residents not to flush grease, food scraps, disposable diapers, condoms, wipes, dental floss or personal hygiene products. Wipes sold as flushable are the usual culprit in a clogged toilet, and they build up with grease further down the line.

If your toilet clogs often, look at what goes down it before you replace anything. A new toilet will clog just as quickly if wipes keep entering the line.

Installing a new toilet

A new toilet is a fair choice when the porcelain is cracked, the toilet is decades old, or repeated repairs have cost more than a replacement. The plumber pulls the old toilet, checks the flange, sets a new wax ring and the new toilet, and tests the flush.

Most houses use a toilet that fits a 12 inch rough-in, but some older homes use 10 or 14 inch, so the measurement is checked first. Toilets rated to the EPA's WaterSense standard use 1.28 gallons per flush or less, which can lower water use over time. For a bathroom remodel or a new fixture run, see plumbing installation.

Wake County's permit guidance says minor repairs or replacement of plumbing fixtures do not need a permit if they do not disrupt the water supply, waste or ventilation systems. Questions about toilet repair in Wake Forest

Do you handle toilet emergencies at night or on weekends in Wake Forest?

Yes. Call (919) 944-7281 any hour. An overflowing toilet or one that cannot be shut off belongs on the 24/7 line. The dispatcher books a plumber for the next opening and you hear the price before work begins.

A running toilet or a slow leak can usually wait for a booked daytime visit.

Why does my toilet keep running?

Usually the flapper at the bottom of the tank no longer seals, or the fill valve does not shut off. Both are inexpensive parts and a plumber can replace either in minutes.

Is a leaking toilet base an emergency?

Not always an emergency, but it should be fixed soon. A leak can soak the subfloor and rot it over time. If water is pooling, shut the supply valve and call.

Can I flush wipes labeled flushable?

Raleigh Water asks residents not to flush wipes. They do not break down like toilet paper and clog lines. Put them in the trash.
