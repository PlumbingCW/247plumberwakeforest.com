What to tell the dispatcher

A few details help the dispatcher send the right plumber with the right parts. You do not need to know the cause. Describe what you can see, hear or smell.

Have these ready when you call.

Your address

Include the street, the town and the ZIP code.

What is happening

Say whether water is running, backing up or leaking, and where.

Whether water is off

Say if you have shut off a fixture valve or the main.

Safety concerns

Mention a gas smell, standing water near electrical items or sewage in living areas.

The best call-back number

A mobile number lets the plumber text or call when on the way.

Do this while you wait

If water is flooding, shut off the main valve, usually near the meter or where the line enters the house. For a toilet that is overflowing, close the valve behind it. For a backup, stop using water. Turn off the power to anything standing in water only if you can do it safely from a dry spot.

If you smell gas, leave the house and call your gas utility from outside. That is a different emergency from a plumbing one, and we cannot handle it by phone.

Where we work

We serve Wake Forest and the nearby town of Rolesville. Our Rolesville page covers ZIP 27571, and the service area page lists the places we cover. The full list of jobs is on our plumbing services page.

Not sure which service fits your problem? Call and describe it. The dispatcher will point you to the right visit, whether it is a drain, a heater, a leak or a fixture.

What happens after you call

The dispatcher confirms your address and the problem, then books a plumber and tells you what to expect. The plumber looks at the problem and explains the options and the price before starting. If the job is bigger than the first call suggested, the plumber says so and you decide how to proceed.

If you are not at the property, tell the dispatcher who will let the plumber in. Keep a phone nearby so the plumber can reach you on the way.

Commercial and after-hours calls

The same line handles shops, restaurants and offices. Tell the dispatcher it is a business and whether the problem is stopping you from opening. Our commercial plumbing page lists the work we do for business owners.

About our emails

Phone is the fastest route, and we answer calls first. An email address is listed for records and general notes. It is not monitored like the phone line, so do not use it for an emergency.
