Water Line Break in Wake Forest, NC: Who Pays for the Repair?

When a water line breaks, the meter is the dividing line. The Town of Wake Forest explains who handles which side.

Call (919) 944-7281

By the Wake Forest 24/7 Plumbing team · Published November 13, 2026

A broken water line can turn a yard into a puddle in an hour. Water may rise through the grass, the meter box may fill or the pressure inside the house may drop to a trickle. The first question after shutting off the water is usually who has to fix it and who has to pay.

This post explains where the Town of Wake Forest draws the line, who to call and how to keep the damage small while you wait.

First steps when a line breaks

Work through these in order. Each one limits damage or gets the right person on the way.

  1. Find the shutoff

    Turn off the valve where the line enters the house if you can reach it safely. If the leak is on the street side of the meter, you may not be able to stop it.

  2. Look at the meter box

    Note whether water is rising in the box and take a photo. Do not open a flooded box if the ground is unstable.

  3. Call the right office

    The Town lists phone numbers for water service. Use the number for your time of day.

  4. Call a plumber

    If the break is on your side, a plumber can locate and repair it.

  5. Document it

    Take photographs and video of the wet area, the meter and any repair work.

Where the Town draws the line

The Town of Wake Forest's website says Raleigh Public Utilities operates Wake Forest's water system. It repairs damage between the street's water main and the meter, and in the main itself. It also says that when pipes break between the water meter and the home, or within the home, the customer is responsible for repairs.

Put simply, the meter is the hinge. Water lines before it belong to the utility. Water lines after it belong to you.

Wake Forest local note: which number to dial

The Town's water service page lists 919-996-4540 for weekdays and 919-829-1930 for nights and weekends. Raleigh Water's Customer Care and Billing line, 919-996-3245, runs around the clock and also takes sewer backup reports.

Keep both on your phone. Call the Town or Raleigh Water to report a break on the utility side. Call a plumber for the customer side. If you are unsure which side, say so on the call and describe where the water is coming up. Source type: Town of Wake Forest water service page and Raleigh Water.

How a plumber finds the break

A line that has failed between the meter and the house is buried, so the first job is to find it. A plumber looks at the pattern of wet ground, listens along the line and checks the meter for movement with all fixtures off. Sometimes the wet spot sits far from the actual break, because water follows the easiest path through soil.

Once the break is found, the repair may be a short section or a longer replacement. Our water service line page explains the options, and a leak detection visit helps when the location is not obvious.

Repair or replace the line

A single split in an otherwise sound line can be repaired. A line with several failures, heavy corrosion or an old material is often better replaced in full. Replacing it means one project instead of repeated digs, and the trench is open once.

Ask your plumber how the old line will be handled, how deep the new one will go and who restores the yard. Those details belong in the quote.

Insurance and documentation

Some homeowner policies cover water line damage and some do not. Others sell it as an add-on. Call your agent after the water is off and ask what they need. Photos and video of the break, the repair and the receipts make a claim go smoother.

What a repair visit looks like

A plumber starts by confirming the break is on your side of the meter. Then the line is exposed with a small trench, the damaged section is cut out and a new section is joined in. The trench is backfilled and the line is tested before the yard is restored.

Trenchless methods exist for some lines, and they can reduce digging under driveways and landscaping. Whether they suit your line depends on its material and condition, so ask your plumber what is possible.

Questions to ask before work starts

Ask whether the quote covers restoring the lawn or pavement. Ask what pipe material will be used and how deep the new line will sit. Ask whether the work needs a permit and who will pull it. Clear answers up front prevent arguments later.

Preventing the next break

Know where your main shutoff is and test it once a year. Keep heavy vehicles off the line and keep large tree roots away from it. If your line is old, an inspection before it fails costs less than an emergency dig.

Questions about water line breaks in Wake Forest

Who do I call at night for a water line break in Wake Forest?

The Town of Wake Forest lists 919-829-1930 for nights and weekends. Raleigh Water's Customer Care line, 919-996-3245, is also staffed around the clock.

Does the Town fix a break between the meter and my house?

The Town's website says the customer is responsible for pipes between the water meter and the home, or inside it. The utility handles the main and the stretch between the main and the meter.
