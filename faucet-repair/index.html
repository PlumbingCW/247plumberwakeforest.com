Faucet that will not shut off: what to do now

A faucet that keeps running after the handle is closed is usually a worn cartridge, a failed ceramic disc or a seat that has pitted. It wastes water by the hour and can overflow a sink if the drain is blocked. The fix is quick once the plumber has the right cartridge.

A split or loose supply line under the sink is more urgent because it can spray with full line pressure. The shutoff valves under the sink isolate it. If a valve is stuck or leaks around the stem, that is a repair of its own and the plumber replaces it while there.

Shower and tub valve leaks

A shower valve that drips after you turn it off, or a tub faucet that leaks when the shower runs, usually has a worn cartridge or stem. The valve sits in the wall, so the repair often means removing the trim and sometimes opening the wall behind it. A plumber matches the cartridge to the brand and model.

A stain on the ceiling below a bathroom, or a wet wall behind the tub, may point to a leak in the valve body or the supply behind it. That is a job for leak detection as well as a valve replacement.

What a drip costs on your Raleigh Water bill

Raleigh Water bills Wake Forest accounts, and its leak adjustment policy treats a leaking faucet as an accessible leak. For those, water charges are adjusted down to twice the monthly average, but wastewater charges are not. The city asks for a plumber's receipt or other proof of repair, and it limits adjustments to one per 12 months.

Small drips rarely qualify, because usage must be at least twice the monthly average, but the receipt for a repair is worth keeping if a drip ran for a long time.

Soft water and what it means for faucets

Raleigh Water's 2025 report lists the water as soft, with about 1.44 to 1.49 grains per gallon. Soft water leaves fewer mineral deposits inside cartridges and aerators than hard water does. Faucets in Wake Forest still wear from use, and old seals harden with age, so a faucet that is 15 or 20 years old may be better replaced than rebuilt.

If the faucet is outdated and the parts are hard to find, a new faucet is often the better value. Installing one is covered under plumbing installation.

Yes. Call (919) 944-7281 any hour. A faucet or valve that will not shut off, or a supply line spraying water, belongs on the 24/7 line. Close the shutoff valves under the sink first if you can.

A slow drip is a booked daytime visit. A spray, a flood or a valve running behind a wall is the one that cannot wait.

Why is my faucet dripping even after I replaced the washer?

The seat inside a compression faucet may be pitted and cut the new washer. A plumber resurfaces or replaces the seat. In a cartridge faucet, the O-rings or the cartridge itself may be at fault.

Can I fix a leaking shower valve myself?

Some people can swap a cartridge, but you need to shut off the water at the right point first, and getting the wrong cartridge is common. If the valve is in a finished wall, a plumber saves time and avoids damage.

Is a leaking faucet worth repairing or should I replace it?

If the faucet is under 10 or 15 years old and parts are available, repair is usually worth it. For older or discontinued models, replacement is often cheaper in the long run.
